v1.1.0 - 2026-09-11
Added: A broken-shield icon flashes above a zombie when its last armor plate breaks, so you can tell it is open to health damage.
Added: Fire, bleed, and stun icons show in a row after the health bar while a zombie is affected, each counting down as the effect wears off.
Added: Settings for the status icon size, the armor-break icon size, and the icon show delay.
Changed: The health bar sits a little higher above the zombie by default, to clear the head.
Changed: Damage numbers merge over a shorter window by default, so quick separate hits show their own numbers.
