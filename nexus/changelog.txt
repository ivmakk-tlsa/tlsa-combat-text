v1.1.1 - 2026-09-11
Fixed: The fire, bleed, stun, and broken-shield icons no longer disappear for the rest of the session after the game unloads assets, such as on a map change.

v1.1.0 - 2026-09-11
Added: A broken-shield icon flashes above the health bar when a zombie's last armor plate breaks.
Added: Fire, bleed, and stun icons appear to the right of the health bar. Each icon turns gray from top to bottom as the effect wears off.
Added: Settings to resize the status and broken-shield icons, and adjust the delay before status icons appear.
Changed: Health bars sit higher above zombies' heads by default.
Changed: The default damage-number merge window is shorter, reduced from 0.5 to 0.1 seconds, so rapid hits are less likely to combine into one number.
